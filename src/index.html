Supported formats and technical details +

Authy GDPR

CSV import with name, encrypted_seed, salt, iv. AES-256-CBC + PBKDF2-SHA1, 100,000 iterations. Use your Authy backup password.

Ente Auth

Import and export plaintext otpauth and encrypted v1 backups. Argon2id + XChaCha20-Poly1305 secretstream. Export: 64 MiB, 3 iterations.

Aegis

Import plain or encrypted JSON; export encrypted v1. scrypt + AES-256-GCM. Open format, also importable in Ente.

Passbolt / KeePass

Password-based KDBX v3/v4 import; encrypted KDBX v3 export. TimeOtp fields and otp URIs. TOTP only: entries without TOTP are counted and excluded. No keyfiles.

Standard TOTP by default. Enable compatibility to import HOTP, Steam and mOTP. Aegis preserves all four types; Ente/plaintext preserve HOTP and Steam but reject mOTP. Passbolt KDBX export remains TOTP-only. Other apps can scan standard QR codes where supported; their proprietary backups are not implemented. Expensive Ente KDF settings may require up to 1 GiB of RAM. Aegis groups, icons and notes are retained in Aegis exports; otpauth conversion focuses on TOTP parameters.

Authy CBC does not authenticate data: always compare the original codes. JavaScript cannot guarantee physical erasure of every copy of a secret in memory.
